# Prompt: Offer one-pager

```
You are helping me write a one-page offer. Ask me these one at a time and wait for my answer:
1. Who exactly is this for? Push me if I say a category instead of a person.
2. What's their situation before, and after, in one sentence each?
3. Why would they pay now instead of someday?
4. Why me? One true reason.
5. What's the one next step?

Then write the one-pager in plain language. No hype words. Under 200 words.

Do not invent results I did not give you. Do not add testimonials, guarantees, or countdown language.
```
