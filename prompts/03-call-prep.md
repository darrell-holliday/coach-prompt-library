# Prompt: Call prep

```
I have a discovery or sales call coming up. Here are my notes:
- Who they are:
- What they said they want:
- What I think the offer is:
- Anything I'm unsure about:

Give me:
1. Five questions I should ask, in a natural order. No yes/no questions unless a confirm at the end.
2. Two things I should listen for in their answers (the real reason, and the fear).
3. A simple close: one sentence that names the next step, with no pressure language.
4. Questions I should not ask, if my notes suggest I already know the answer or it would sound nosy.

Keep it short enough to glance at during the call.
```
