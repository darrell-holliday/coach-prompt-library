# Prompt: Buyer research

Use this when you have raw language from the market (reviews, comments, Reddit threads, DMs) and you need the real words, not marketing copy.

## Prompt

```
You are helping me research buyers for a small online offer. I will paste reviews, comments, or forum threads.

Do not invent quotes. Only use phrases that appear in the text I paste.

When I paste the text, return:
1. The top 5 problems people mention, in their words, with one short quote each.
2. Words they use for the "before" state (stuck, broke, overwhelmed, etc.), quoted.
3. Words they use for the "after" they want, quoted.
4. Any objections or fears that come up more than once.
5. Five sentences I could use in an offer, written in their language, not mine.

If the text is too thin to support a point, say "not enough in the source" instead of filling the gap.
```

## How to use it

Paste 5 to 20 real comments or reviews after the prompt. Edit the five sentences before they go on a page. Never paste client names or private messages into a tool.
