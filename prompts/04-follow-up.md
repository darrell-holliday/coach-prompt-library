# Prompt: Follow-up drafts

```
Draft a short follow-up I will send myself. Do not address it as if you are sending it.

Context:
- Their name:
- What we talked about:
- Where they left it ("need to think", "talk to a partner", "interested, no date"):
- The next step I want to offer:
- Tone: plain, direct, no pep talk, no "just circling back" filler.

Write three options:
1. Two sentences.
2. A four-sentence version that includes one useful recap of their goal.
3. A version for if they already said no, that leaves the door open without asking twice in a needy way.

I will edit and send one of these myself.
```
