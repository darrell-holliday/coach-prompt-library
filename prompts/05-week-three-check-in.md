# Prompt: Week-three check-in (for coaches)

```
A client is at the "this feels pointless" point, usually around week three. Here are my notes:
- What they committed to:
- What they actually did:
- How they sounded on the last call:
- The 90-day number we set:

Help me prep a 20-minute check-in:
1. One sentence that names the slump as normal, without sounding fake-cheerful.
2. Three tight questions to find out if this is fear, a fuzzy offer, or they just didn't do the work.
3. One shrunk goal for the next seven days. Small enough that they can't talk themselves out of it.
4. What I should not do: no guilt, no piling on more tasks, no new tools.

Speak as notes to me, the coach, not as a message to the client. I will use my own words on the call.
```

This prompt is generic coaching practice. Do not paste anything from a private coaching platform, and do not include a client's real name.
