# Example: a filled offer one-pager

This is a made-up example so you can see the shape. It is not a real client or a real offer.

**Answers given to the prompt**

1. Who: Independent personal trainers who already train clients in person and want a simple online program to sell in the off-season.
2. Before: They only get paid when they are in the room. After: they have a $500 off-season program they can point people to, and they've sold it at least five times.
3. Why now: Their in-person book drops every December and they want this ready before it happens again.
4. Why them: They've trained 200+ clients in person and already have the workouts. They don't need more content. They need an offer and a way to sell it.
5. Next step: A 30-minute call.

**One-pager the prompt might draft (edited)**

You're a personal trainer who gets paid when you're in the room, and every December the book goes quiet.

I help trainers like you turn the work you already do into a simple $500 off-season program, and sell it through real conversations, not a giant funnel.

In 12 weeks we pick the offer, talk to people who already know you, and get the first sales by hand. Then we set up a follow-up routine you can keep running when you're back on the floor.

No audience of 10,000 required. No income promises. If you've already trained clients in person and you want a second way to get paid this winter, book a 30-minute call.
